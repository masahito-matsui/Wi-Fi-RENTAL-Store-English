Rental Extension

 

The rental rates for extension are same as the regular rental rates.

Place your extension order by selecting the model and duration from the following list.

- The extension order must be placed on or before your rental end date.
- You can continue to use the current Wi-Fi router by placing an extension order.
- If an extension order is not placed by the rental end date, a late fee will be charged at 864880 yen per day.
For au KDDI routers, the late fee is 1,0801,100 yen per day (tax inclusive).

Data Plan

Even if you extend your rental period in the middle of the month, you don't get more data.
Data is reset on 1st of every calendar month.
