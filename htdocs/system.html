Rental System

Our Rental System

 

The start date for the rental period is the specified delivery date of the router.
Please send back the return envelope by 8AM the next day of the rental end date.
We deliver your order to wherever you like, and you can send it back from anywhere in Japan.

Delivery

 

Airport

You can pick up your Wi-Fi device upon your arrival to Japan.

Hotel

We deliver your parcel to your hotel reception.
Simply pick it up when you check-in.

Friend’s Home / Airbnb
/ Company Dorm

We deliver to where your are staying.
Your parcel will be delivered directly to you by the courier.

In-Store Pickup
in Akihabara, Tokyo

You can pick up your device at our store, too.

For further information, please see Receive and Return page.

For orders placed by 5PM, next day delivery is available.

Return

 

Please send back the rental items using the enclosed return envelope and by posting it into any postbox.

You can send back the rental items from any postbox in Japan.
* There are postboxes at aiports, too!

The return envelope is included with the Wi-Fi router in the rental set you receive.

Please review the order details, and inform us of any inaccuracies as soon as possible.

3 . Check what is inside the pouch.

- Wi-Fi router main unit
- AC adapter
- USB cable

Wi-Fi routers will be fully charged before we deliver it to you. You can use it upon your receipt.

5.Return

Please send back the rental set by placing it in the "Letter Pack" (the return envelope)
You will need to post it by 8AM the next day of the end date.

Please put the rental set into the return envelope.

Then place it in a postbox by the end of the rental period, and you're done!

The rental ends when you post it into postbox.
A confirmation email will be sent within a week after your return of the rental items.
