Same Day Delivery
Available on weekdays, in central Tokyo

 

In central Tokyo (23 wards), a few options for same day delivery is available

If you require same day delivery, please contact us by phone. Our ordering system does not allow same day delivery.

We can deliver the same day to your specified location in central Tokyo.

Same day delivery via Yu-Pack (ゆうパック)

Delivery only on weekdays to limited areas in central Tokyo.

* You are required to call us after placing your online order.

(An express fee of 540550 yen will be charged in addition to the delivery fee.)
