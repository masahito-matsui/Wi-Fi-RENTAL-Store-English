Pickup at airports

As our service, we deliver your order to post offices and delivery counters at airports. We have been chosen by a large amount of foreign travelers so far.

In the case of pickup at airports, advance order is required.
Please place an order two days before your arrival/pickup date.

You can connect
right away!

Receipt at airports makes you connect to the internet right away
after your arrival in Japan.
The parcel arrives one day earlier than your designated pickup date.
You can pick up right after your arrival.
*During business hours only.

Much more economical than the rental at airports.

When you apply WiFi rental at airports,
it tends to cost more than 1,000 yen per a day.
Our rental price costs less than a half of the rental price at airports.

※Airport handling fee: 540 yen is required in addition.
※Please refer to the bottom of this page for the list of post offices and delivery counters.

The order method

How to place an order for customers who wish to pick up at airports.

① Add an item into your cart.
　 Select "Pickup Method" as "At airport" when you see the contents of the shopping cart.

② At "Customer information input" page, select a post office or a delivery counter.

・Please confirm the business hours of the post office and the delivery counter surely.
　You can NOT pick up before/after business hours.

・They are closed on weekends and Japanese holidays
at Haneda Airport post office and Chubu International Airport post office (Centrair post office).

・As for Haneda Airport post office, domestic terminal is only available.
　You can NOT pick up at international terminal.

The pickup method

How to receive at post offices and delivery counters.

After your arrival, visit the selected pickup place. Please tell the staff that you are receiving WiFi routers. They identify by your passport information.

The office workers are accustomed to the pickup service.
Please just inform that you are receiving WiFi routers then they will arrange it.
